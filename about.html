Meet the Director

Hi, I am Joy Zhou.

Joy Zhou is a music educator, researcher, and founder of Be You Music. She holds four degrees in vocal performance and music education from top universities across China, the United States, and Canada.

Her teaching journey includes leading comprehensive music programs at an elite international school in Beijing and serving as a certified Montessori teacher at a historic school in downtown Toronto.

Joy's work at the University of Ottawa's Music and Health Research Institute explores how music impacts the human brain and wellness across a lifetime. That scientific perspective shapes a high-level, child-centered studio experience.

Built From the Inside Out

Students feel the music before they sing it.

Be You Music uniquely integrates movement into choral and private study. Dalcroze Eurhythmics and Montessori principles help children internalize rhythm, build confidence, and connect naturally to ensemble singing.
